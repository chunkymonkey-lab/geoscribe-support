Survey records and permissions

Depending on the features you use, local app storage contains routes, timestamps, location-quality estimates, speed and altitude readings, boundaries, measurements, points, notes, photographs, recordings, transcripts and reports.

Location
Shows your position and records routes. Explicit tracking can continue in the background with the required permission.
Camera and photos
Attach captured or selected images to observations.
Microphone
Records voice notes after an explicit start, with visible recording status.
Speech recognition
Transcribes recordings using Apple's speech services, which can include remote processing.

Change permissions in device Settings. Withdrawal prevents associated future access, not deletion of existing records. Do not record other people or sensitive information without appropriate authority.

Exports can contain precise locations, names and media. Review them before sharing. Sharing creates copies governed by the recipient or destination service; backups and file-sync services are separate from GeoScribe. The app does not automatically publish your survey on this website.

Maps and online requests

Map display, search, routing, coordinate conversion and offline preparation make requests to selected services. Depending on your choices, these include Apple Maps, OpenStreetMap Foundation, Ordnance Survey, Mapbox, what3words, OpenFreeMap, and the Private.coffee or overpass-api.de Overpass servers.

These services receive your connection IP address and request information such as search text, coordinates, tile indices or survey bounds. Requested areas need not be your current location. Provider keys or tokens can link requests to a provider account. User-entered keys are stored in Keychain and transmitted to the relevant provider for authentication.

Providers may retain request or security logs. For example, what3words describes API-call content, time, key and IP logging and analysis of API usage. Provider retention, onward recipients and processing locations are governed by the applicable service terms, not by the lifetime of a local survey. GeoScribe does not promise anonymous requests or immediate deletion by providers.

Pro offline-map preparation can request OpenFreeMap tiles when a survey finishes if the setting is enabled, as well as for explicit area downloads. This sends the requested area, not notes, photos or audio. Turn off automatic preparation in Offline reports settings. A saved street map does not make search, satellite imagery or navigation offline.

HTML reports embed their exported survey records. Successfully embedded street-map extracts need no tile-server connection. Optional online layers require the reader's confirmation. External Google Maps links send the selected coordinate to Google when opened.

Provider notices: Apple Maps, OpenStreetMap Foundation, Ordnance Survey, Mapbox, what3words, OpenFreeMap, Private.coffee, overpass-api.de operator information and Google. The overpass-api.de link is operator information, not a promise of a specific retention period.

Voice notes and transcription

Recordings are saved locally. Transcription first attempts on-device Apple recognition where supported. If that is unavailable or unsuccessful, standard Apple Speech recognition may send the audio to Apple. Transcription is not guaranteed to remain offline.

Finishing a recording starts transcription; export can retry pending, partial or failed transcripts. The original recording is retained when transcription fails. Review the result before relying on it. Speech Recognition permission can be withdrawn in Settings > Privacy & Security > Speech Recognition.

Apple describes third-party transcription in Siri, Dictation & Privacy. GeoScribe's developer does not operate the speech servers or set Apple's retention policy.

Purchases and access

Apple handles payments. The app checks Apple's signed subscription and original-download information locally to determine Pro access, including eligible earlier paid buyers. The app does not send payment-card details to the developer and has no developer-operated entitlement server.

Restore Purchases is in the Pro screen. Cancel or manage subscriptions in your Apple Account. Deleting the app does not cancel renewal.

Keeping and deleting information

  • Named surveys remain until deleted. Deleting a saved job removes its job file and list entry, not necessarily all linked media or exports.
  • Images and audio are stored separately, without a general age-based purge.
  • Automatic-save cleanup retains up to five copies. Session, recovery and automatic-save files older than seven days are eligible for cleanup when the persistence service starts; this is not an exact-time deletion guarantee.
  • Delete downloaded map areas in Settings > Offline map areas. Temporary map caches may be pruned by version or storage limits.
  • Manage exports, recipient copies and backups separately. Deleting app data cannot recall files already shared.

Preserve important records before deleting data. Removing permissions does not erase previous records.

Support, legal bases and your rights

Support uses iCloud Mail. Sending an enquiry gives the developer your sender address, message and any attachments you choose to include. Support emails are deleted after three months. This does not set a deletion period for independently retained provider backups or copies you shared elsewhere. Do not send passwords, keys or unnecessary survey records. Ask for any separately saved troubleshooting material to be deleted when your issue is resolved.

We process information needed to provide requested app functionality and purchase access to perform our agreement with you. We handle enquiries and necessary troubleshooting in our legitimate interests of responding to users and maintaining a functioning app. Where consent is required for an optional use, you can refuse or withdraw it; doing so does not undo previous lawful processing. We may process information where necessary to comply with legal obligations. Device permissions control technical access and are not blanket consent to unrelated processing.

You need not email support or supply optional recordings and photos. Without information needed to investigate a particular issue, we may be unable to resolve it. The app does not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

Apple, GitHub and other providers may process information outside the UK under their own notices and transfer arrangements. Their policies explain applicable safeguards and contact routes; we do not claim all processing stays in the UK. See Apple's privacy policy for mail and other Apple services and the provider links above.

Contact geoscribe.support@icloud.com to ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or to object, subject to applicable legal conditions. We cannot retrieve files held only on your device or delete a separate provider's records ourselves. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

This website and changes

This GitHub Pages website has no analytics script, ads, embedded map, external font or contact form. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security under its privacy statement. Following a link or sending email uses the named external service.

We will date updates here and describe material changes in relevant app release information before they take effect. Any new processing requiring consent will require that consent separately. This policy describes handling of information; it does not transfer ownership of your survey content.
